The **Nonlinear Complex System Laboratory** is led by **Prof. Pilwon Kim** in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at UNIST.

We study how complex phenomena emerge, evolve, and interact. Our work combines dynamical systems theory, mathematical modeling, numerical computation, and machine learning to understand nonlinear behavior across science, engineering, and social systems.

## Research philosophy

Complexity does not mean that systems are beyond understanding. By connecting rigorous mathematics with computation and AI, we seek structure within apparently irregular behavior and develop tools for analysis, prediction, and control.
