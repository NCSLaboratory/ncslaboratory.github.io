NCSLaboratory is a research group focused on the foundations and deployment of dependable artificial intelligence.

We study how intelligent systems can remain reliable when data is incomplete, environments change, and decisions carry meaningful consequences. Our work combines machine learning, statistics, systems engineering, and human-centered design.

## Our mission

To develop AI systems that people can understand, evaluate, and trust in the settings where reliability matters most.
