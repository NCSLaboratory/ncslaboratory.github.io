About

Researching the foundations of dependable AI.

NCSLaboratory brings together researchers, engineers, and students working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and safety-critical system design. Our mission is to create AI systems that are powerful, robust, transparent, and accountable in real deployment settings.

Research

Key areas of investigation

01

AI Safety

Methods for reducing failure modes, improving robustness, and enabling safer machine behavior under uncertainty.

02

Trustworthy ML

Robust training, calibration, and interpretability for models that communicate uncertainty and maintain reliability.

03

Decision Intelligence

Adaptive systems that translate data into trustworthy decisions using principled statistical and learning techniques.

04

Human-Centered AI

AI systems that improve collaboration while preserving accountability, transparency, and human agency.
